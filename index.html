AI assessment platform

I integrated Gemini through Vertex AI to generate assessment questions and built recovery so generation can resume after a restart and retry incomplete sections. I worked across the candidate experience and review process, including hints, auto-submission, reattempts, scoring rules, and approval routing.

Adaptive mock interviews

I helped build an AI interviewer that uses job context to ask role-specific questions and adapts to spoken answers. I worked on candidate recordings, feedback, scoring, and recovery when generation or speech processing fails. I also scaled text-to-speech for concurrent interview sessions and bounded speech worker retries.

Bulk processing at scale

I built RabbitMQ-backed workers to import and update candidate records and documents from CSV and XLSX files. I increased consumer concurrency, added recovery for queued chunks, and fixed a recovery path that could flood the queue. Durable status, duplicate detection, and row-level errors give operators a way to inspect and recover interrupted imports.

Recruiter analytics

I optimized dashboard and export APIs for placement cycles, candidate stages, salary metrics, and assessment results using MongoDB aggregations and projections. I added agency and role filters and PostgreSQL-backed audit trails for actions such as publishing a job.
